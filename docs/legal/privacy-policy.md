# TideTrace Privacy Policy

**Draft for review — effective date: 15 October 2026**

TideTrace operates a community conservation platform from the Philippines and acts as the personal information controller for the information described in this policy. This policy explains what information we process, why we process it, who may receive it, and how you can exercise your rights under applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

## 1. Information we process

**Account information.** We process your email address, display name, account identifier, authentication information, account role and status, and an optional profile photo. Passwords are processed through our authentication service. We also process verification, recovery, and session information to manage access.

**Contributions and interactions.** We store Trace titles, descriptions, categories, location names, optional coordinates, uploaded media, submission status, and timestamps. We also process comments, content reports, moderation decisions and feedback, notifications and related preferences, and lesson-completion records. Information you send when requesting assistance may also be retained to resolve your request.

**Location and media.** Location names and map pins are supplied by you. The current map feature lets you choose a pin; it does not require continuous device-location tracking. Images and other uploads may contain identifiable people or embedded metadata, including location information. Do not assume that all metadata is removed before storage or publication.

**Technical information.** Operating the service involves processing IP addresses, request details, session identifiers, and security events. Hosting and embedded-service providers may also receive browser and device information and maintain their own operational logs. TideTrace uses IP-based request limits to reduce abuse.

We receive information directly from you, from your use of the service, from authentication and hosting services, and from other users when they submit content or reports involving you.

## 2. Why we use information and our lawful bases

We use information to:

- Create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and support account recovery.
- Save, review, and publish contributions and support community discussion.
- Deliver lessons, save completion records, and provide service notifications.
- Investigate reports, enforce community rules, maintain audit records, and protect the service.
- Respond to requests and meet applicable legal obligations.
- Understand platform activity through internal operational statistics.

Depending on the activity, we rely on processing necessary to provide the service under our agreement with you, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests in security and responsible community operation, balanced against your rights. Where consent is required, we will seek it for the specific purpose. For sensitive personal information, we must identify an applicable lawful basis under the stricter rules governing that information before processing it.

Providing information essential to an account or submission is necessary to use that feature. A profile photo and precise map coordinates are optional. Declining optional information does not prevent use of unrelated features.

This policy provides notice; reading it or accepting the Terms and Conditions is not blanket consent to every use of personal information.

## 3. What other people can see

Approved, visible Traces can be viewed by the public, including visitors without accounts. Public content may include your display name, profile photo, contribution text, uploaded evidence, location name, and any saved coordinates. Visible comments may also show your display name and profile photo.

Drafts and non-public submissions are subject to access controls. Authorized moderators and administrators may access information needed for review, account administration, security, or complaint handling. Email addresses, authentication credentials, reports, and private account records are not intended for public display.

Public material may be copied, indexed, or redistributed by other people. Consider using a display name that does not reveal your full identity, and avoid sharing private addresses, unnecessary personal details, or sensitive wildlife locations.

## 5. Browser storage and external technologies

TideTrace uses browser storage to maintain sign-in sessions and remember your display-theme preference. Depending on browser support, session information may persist across visits, remain only for a tab, or remain only in memory. Passwords are not saved in this session storage. Signing out clears the application's local session state; clearing browser data can also remove saved preferences and require you to sign in again.

Embedded services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their practices and any required consent controls must be considered separately from TideTrace's essential session storage.

## 7. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including account operation, publication, security, dispute resolution, and legal requirements. Retention depends on the type of record, whether the account or contribution remains active, and any applicable legal obligation.

**Retention schedule: [insert approved periods or specific criteria for accounts, drafts and published content, reports and audit records, support requests, technical logs, unused uploads, and backups].**

When processing a request, we will explain any lawful reason to retain particular records and restrict their use as appropriate. Backup copies may remain until their scheduled expiry. Copies independently retained by other people are outside our direct control.

## 8. Security

TideTrace uses authenticated access, role-based permissions, database access controls, and restricted file access to help protect information. Access links for protected media are time-limited, although approved public evidence can be viewed by visitors. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your credentials private and use care on shared devices. We will handle security incidents and any required notifications in accordance with applicable law.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, you may request information about processing, access to your personal information, correction of inaccuracies, objection to processing, erasure or blocking, and data portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent for processing that depends on consent, without affecting processing lawfully performed before withdrawal. You may also seek damages where legally available and lodge a complaint with the [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/).

You can edit supported profile details and manage available preferences in the app. For other requests, follow the guidance in Section 12 and describe what you need. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity. We will respond within applicable legal requirements and explain any lawful restriction on fulfilling your request. An account restriction does not remove your privacy rights.

## 10. Children and information about other people

Users under 18 must have the involvement and agreement of a parent or legal guardian as described in our Terms and Conditions. Where legally required, appropriate parental or guardian authorization must be obtained before processing a child's information. A parent or guardian may contact TideTrace about a child's information or request review or removal.

Do not upload unnecessary personal information about another person. If an upload identifies someone, ensure you have an appropriate lawful basis and any required permission, particularly for images of children.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We will publish updates with a revised effective date and provide appropriate notice of material changes through the platform. If a new use requires consent or another legal step, we will complete that step before proceeding.

## 12. Privacy inquiries and requests

Privacy inquiries, complaints, and requests to exercise your rights should describe the information or processing involved and the action you are requesting. We may seek proportionate information to verify your identity before disclosing or changing personal information. Never include passwords or verification codes.

Where a privacy or support channel is made available on the platform, use it to submit your request. Avoid posting sensitive information in public Traces or comments. These procedures do not limit your right to raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission or exercise other rights under applicable law.
